| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-1-39 | Elevation of privilege | workflow token and triggers (VALIDATION T-1-ci) | high | mitigate | `permissions: contents: read`; trigger `pull_request` only (no target-context trigger); `persist-credentials: false`; policy check in `yarn verify` forbids write permissions, `id-token` and target-context triggers |
